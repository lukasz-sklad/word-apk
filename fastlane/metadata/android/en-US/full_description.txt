WordCraft (Android) is an unofficial, 100% offline Android fork based on WordCraft (storytold/wordcraft), written in pure Rust and Kotlin.

Key features:
- Opens and saves .docx (OOXML), .odt, .rtf, .html, .md and .txt; opens legacy .doc files; exports real selectable PDFs.
- Touch-adapted toolbar in Polish (PL) and English (EN) with safe-area cutout / freeform window detection.
- Gboard / IME composition and autocorrect support, voice dictation via system keyboard, single-finger selection mode (Sel/Zazn), and two-finger pinch zoom.
- Bundled offline Polish Hunspell dictionary (pl_PL) and TeX hyphenation patterns alongside English proofing.
- Privacy first: zero INTERNET permission, no Google Play Services, no trackers, no ads, and no background auto-updater.
